Number style

Block numbers stay on each block and follow the first column and first row. In order prints 1, 2, 3 straight across the card. One hand uses only the digits 1–5, so a coach can signal on one hand.

Readability

Field keeps the wristband colors. Shade rows is off until you turn it on. Sun, black & white, trim, and row shading change this print only. They do not change the saved signs.

Versions

New version copies this one and does not shuffle. Shuffle this card mixes only the offense or defense card you are editing. Lock freezes both cards on that version.

    Offense calls

    Offense and defense fill the card. Pitches and locations stay in the library. Weight 1 fills one or two boxes, and a higher weight fills more. Leave weight blank to keep that call’s box count. Shuffle deals from the weights.
