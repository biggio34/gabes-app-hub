Youth is 4.5 × 2.5. Triple windows: 5 × 3 adult, 3.5 × 2.75 youth, 2.75 × 2.15 pee wee, 4.75 × 2.25 panel. Type any other width and height. The card prints at that size.

Number style

Block numbers stay on each block and follow the first column and first row. In order prints 1, 2, 3 straight across the card. One hand uses only the digits 1–5, so a coach can signal on one hand.

Readability

Field keeps the wristband colors. Shade rows and laminate are off until you turn them on. Laminate adds a 1/8 in clear border and puts the cut line outside the print, so the printed card stays the window size after you laminate. Sun, black & white, trim, laminate, and row shading change this print only. They do not change the saved signs.

Versions

Shuffle this card keeps this version and adds the next one with a new mix of the offense or defense card you are editing. New version copies this one and does not shuffle. Reprint prints that saved card. Lock freezes both cards on that version.

    Offense calls

    Offense and defense fill the card. Pitches and locations stay in the library. Weight 1 fills one or two boxes, and a higher weight fills more. Leave weight blank to keep that call’s box count. Shuffle deals from the weights.
