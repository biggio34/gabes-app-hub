What happened to one-click Sync now?

The old button was not this website clicking Facebook for you. It was a helper program that used to live on the salon Mac and poke around in Chrome while you were already signed in. That program is not on this website, and a website is not allowed to control Chrome on your computer. So this page does the part it can: format the Google post and get you to the right tabs.
