Roth IRA Direct Contribution Income Limits (2026)

• Single / Head of Household: $153,000 – $168,000 MAGI
• Married Filing Jointly: $242,000 – $252,000 MAGI

Above these ranges, direct Roth IRA contributions are not allowed (backdoor Roth still available for most).

Important 2026 Notes
  • Higher catch-up contributions for ages 60-63 remain available ($11,250) if your plan permits.
  • Starting in 2026, catch-up contributions for employees earning over $150,000 (indexed) in the prior year must generally be made as Roth (after-tax) contributions.
  • Backdoor Roth IRA strategy remains available for high earners (no income limit on conversions).
